End-to-end sample-level sensitivity at 30x sequencing depth. A: 200 STR loci, b=10%; B: 200 STR loci, b=30%; C: 15 common STR loci, b=10%; D: 15 common STR loci, b=30%. b is the proportion of altered STR loci. Each condition includes the same 200 test donors. Bars indicate the fraction classified as MSI using frozen independently calibrated thresholds. Unresolved samples remain in the denominator. Error bars are Wilson 95% confidence intervals. MSIsensor2 is evaluated in the common-locus panels C and D. Tumor purity follows the existing diploid, clonal, heterozygous simulation convention.
